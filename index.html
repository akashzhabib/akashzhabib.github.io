Ph.D. Student, Electrical Engineering @ West Virginia University

I am a Ph.D. student in Electrical Engineering at West Virginia University, advised by Dr. Prashnna K. Gyawali, where I also earned my M.S. in Electrical Engineering in 2026. I received my B.Sc. in Electrical and Electronic Engineering from BUET, where I worked with Prof. Md. Kamrul Hasan on deep learning for ultrasound shear-wave elastography in collaboration with Fujifilm.

My research focuses on the mechanistic interpretability, evaluation, and post-training of vision–language models (VLMs). I study whether reinforcement-learning post-training gives models genuine visual grounding or merely amplifies language priors, using activation patching, causal tracing, and sparse autoencoders. I also build diagnostic benchmarks that expose failures hidden by aggregate metrics, and develop methods for trustworthy, fair VLMs in medical imaging.

When I’m not probing models, I’m usually busy with photography. Feel free to reach out by email or LinkedIn.
